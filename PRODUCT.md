# Product

## Register

product

## Users

D&D 5e players. The sheet is bilingual: English and Traditional Chinese. They build a character between sessions and run that same character at the table. The main job is a fast glance: hit points, attacks, spells, and dice.

## Product Purpose

A 5e character library and sheet. Create, import, play, and level up. Success: the player reads the number they need and rolls without fighting the chrome.

## Brand Personality

Dark, framed, precise. The reference is the Baldur's Gate 3 interface: dark panels, metal frames, crimson. The game's HUD, not its marketing art. Labels stay short. The chrome does not tell lore.

## Anti-references

Copied Wizards of the Coast art, the dragon logo, and pasted official book pages. Frames and type are original.

## Design Principles

1. Glance beats atmosphere. The number the player needs is the loudest thing in the panel.
2. One sheet for prep and play. No separate pretty mode.
3. Evoke a game HUD. Do not reproduce official marks or book pages.
4. Chrome is quiet metal. Crimson marks an action or the current selection, not decoration.
5. Dim room and daylight both stay readable. Contrast holds in both.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA. Body text, labels, and placeholders at least 4.5:1 against their background. Honor `prefers-reduced-motion`.
